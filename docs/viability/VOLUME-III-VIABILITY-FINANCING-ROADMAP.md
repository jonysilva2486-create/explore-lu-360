### Evidence rule

No financial amount, funding programme, eligibility condition or revenue assumption may be presented as an established fact unless it is verifiable.

The volume must clearly distinguish between:

- approved project decisions;
- estimates;
- hypotheses/scenarios;
- externally verified facts.

For public or institutional funding, preference must be given to current official sources.

# 1. Objet et rôle du volume

Define why Volume III exists, what it must demonstrate and how it relates to Volumes I and II without repeating product vision or software architecture.

Core question: under what conditions can Explore Luxembourg 360 be realised, financed, maintained and developed sustainably?

---

# 2. Principes de viabilité du projet

Define the project's economic and sustainability principles: controlled initial costs, progressive growth, technological independence where appropriate, public accessibility, and avoidance of dependence on hypothetical revenue.

---

# 3. Phases de développement et niveaux de maturité

Separate the project's maturity stages so that costs and financing needs are not treated as if they were identical throughout development:

POC → pilote territorial → première exploitation réelle → extension → déploiement national.

---

# 4. Structure des coûts

Identify the complete cost structure before assigning financial values: digital infrastructure, domain, hosting, storage, APIs, equipment, field capture, transport, maintenance, insurance, communication, development, content updates and other relevant costs.

---

# 5. Budget minimal de fonctionnement

Determine the minimum realistic cost of keeping Explore Luxembourg 360 operational, particularly during the period in which development and field capture are primarily carried out by the project owner.

---

# 6. Scénarios financiers

Build distinct financial scenarios rather than one artificial global budget:

- projet personnel / POC;
- pilote financé;
- exploitation structurée;
- croissance territoriale.

Each scenario must identify its costs, needs, assumptions and risks.

---

# 7. Stratégie de financement public

Assess FEDER, LEADER and other relevant Luxembourgish or European funding possibilities. Clearly distinguish potentially relevant programmes from programmes for which the project and/or future project carrier is actually eligible.

---

# 8. Structure juridique et porteur du projet

Assess when and whether the current individual project requires an association, company, institutional partnership or another legal/operational structure to receive funding and operate sustainably. Do not decide the structure in advance of the analysis.

---

# 9. Modèle économique et sources de revenus

Assess revenue models compatible with the product and its public-facing purpose only after understanding costs and public funding. Potential areas may include territorial services, 360° content/services, collaborations with communes or operators, professional functionality and promotion of relevant experiences, without turning the platform into an advertising catalogue.

---

# 10. Partenariats et contributions non financières

Identify non-financial contributions that can materially reduce project costs or improve feasibility: public data, technical support, permissions, communication, territorial access, institutional collaboration, information provision and similar contributions.

---

# 11. Pérennité et coûts de maintenance

Address what happens after initial construction or funding: route/content updates, obsolete information, equipment renewal, infrastructure, growing storage, technical maintenance and operational continuity.

---

# 12. Analyse des risques financiers et opérationnels

Identify and mitigate risks such as funding dependency, uncontrolled cost growth, insufficient revenue, provider dependency, excessive reliance on one person, maintenance burden and premature expansion.

---

# 13. Stratégie de déploiement progressif

Demonstrate that the project does not need to finance or capture the whole of Luxembourg at once. Define a progressive territorial validation model: prove the concept on a limited scope, measure results, learn, then expand.

---

# 14. Indicateurs de viabilité et critères de décision

Define measurable criteria for decisions such as whether the pilot works, whether expansion is justified, when external funding becomes necessary and when a cost or operational burden becomes unsustainable.

---

# 15. Feuille de route financière

Create a chronological financial path linking the current project to future maturity:

current state → POC → pilot → funding application → financing → expansion → sustainable operation.

---

# 16. Conclusion — vers un modèle durable

Provide a concise institutional conclusion showing how the proposed financial, operational and institutional model can support the long-term development of Explore Luxembourg 360.
