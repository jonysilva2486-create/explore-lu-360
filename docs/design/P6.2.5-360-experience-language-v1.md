# P6.2.5 — 360° Experience Language v1

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Phase:** P6.2 — Component Language

## Decision

**360° is not a feature. It is an experience layer.**

The immersive experience is visually dominated by the panorama while interface controls remain minimal, contextual and subordinate to the place being explored.

## Initial content reality

The platform is designed for a complete 360° experience model, but launch content must reflect what Explore Luxembourg 360 can realistically produce.

Initial content priorities:

- routes, using Google Street View where appropriate and legally/technically suitable;
- own photography of viewpoints, landscapes and selected places;
- exterior documentation of monuments and places of interest.

Future content may add:

- Explore Luxembourg 360-produced 360° panoramas;
- interior monument experiences;
- partner/institutional experiences;
- richer immersive storytelling.

The product must be built for the full experience while launching with content that can realistically be produced.

## Experience model

**Place → Enter 360° → Explore → Contextual hotspot / information → Continue → Related place**

The immersive experience must not become a dead end.

## Interface principles

- Panorama dominates the screen.
- Controls are few and task-focused.
- Information appears contextually rather than as a permanent panel.
- Hotspots are optional and visually discreet.
- Storytelling is progressive.
- Navigation between mapped/immersive points is spatial or narrative, not a substitute for GPS navigation.
- The visual language must not imitate the Google Street View interface even when Street View is the content source.

## Content sources and architecture

The system should distinguish the source/experience type without forcing different user experiences:

- `StreetViewExperience`
- `Explore360Experience`
- `Interior360Experience`
- `PhotoExperience`

The technical 360° engine must be separated from the Explore Experience Layer. This allows the underlying viewer technology to evolve without rebuilding Places, Stories, Hotspots, relationships or the visual language.

## Responsive behaviour

### Mobile

Prioritise immersive viewing, natural gestures and a small number of contextual controls.

### Desktop

Use available space for immersive viewing while allowing contextual navigation, information and route/timeline elements where useful.

## Future readiness

The experience must be compatible with the approved PWA-first strategy and capable of evolving toward a native application without requiring the product experience to be rebuilt from scratch.

## Explicit exclusions

MVP does not require interior mapping, VR, thousands of proprietary panoramas, AI-generated experiences or a full national 360° coverage programme.

## Core principle

> **Build for the full experience. Launch with the content we can realistically produce.**

## Traceability

This decision is subordinate to the approved Product Vision, PDR-005 Screen Inventory + MVP User Flows, PDR-006 Visual Language and the existing architecture/product decisions.
