# P6.2 — Content Strategy Reality v1

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 2026-08-18

## Purpose

Record the realistic content-production boundary that must guide P6.2 design without reducing the long-term product vision.

## Launch content priorities

### Routes

Routes are the initial content backbone. Where appropriate and permitted, Google Street View can provide the visual coverage needed for route exploration, avoiding unnecessary production of imagery that already exists and is suitable.

### Viewpoints and landscapes

Explore Luxembourg 360 will progressively produce its own photography for viewpoints, landscapes and selected places. This content provides a distinctive visual identity and reduces unnecessary dependence on stock imagery.

### Monuments and places of interest

Initial documentation focuses on exterior views. Future relationships with communes, institutions, owners and other partners may enable interior mapping and richer 360° experiences.

## Product requirement

The interface and architecture must already support the future model even when launch content does not contain every media type.

A Place may therefore evolve from:

**Location + photo + route/Street View**

to:

**Location + photos + 360° + interior 360° + stories + related places + partner content**

without requiring a new product architecture.

## Principle

> **Build for the full experience. Launch with the content we can realistically produce.**

## Scope guard

This document does not authorise future content production by itself. Interior mapping, partner access and advanced 360° capture remain dependent on permissions, relationships, resources and later production decisions.
