## Numbering correction

During project work, Motion was temporarily recorded as P6.3.4 and Typography & Content Hierarchy as P6.3.5. This was a numbering drift from the canonical plan.

The correction is now:

- Motion is canonically **P6.3.5**.
- Typography & Content Hierarchy is preserved as a **P6.1 refinement**.
- P6.3.4 remains correctly reserved for **Light / Dark Behaviour**.
- P6.3.3 remains correctly reserved for **System States**.

No approved design work is discarded by this correction.

See `P6.3-reconciliation-register.md` for the complete correction record.

## Brand reconciliation gate

PDR-006 requires the existing official Explore Luxembourg 360 brand assets to be reconciled before final production tokens are frozen. No replacement logo or silent identity change is permitted.

## Content reality constraint

All design-system decisions must remain compatible with the actual initial content strategy:

- routes using Google Street View where appropriate;
- own photography of viewpoints, landscapes and places;
- exterior monument content initially;
- future own 360° production and interior mapping through permissions and collaborations.

The platform must be prepared for the complete future experience without assuming that all advanced content exists at launch.

## Quality gate

Before starting or closing any P6.3 sub-point:

1. verify the point against the canonical plan;
2. present the proposal and explain the reasoning in accessible language;
3. provide a visual demonstration where useful;
4. obtain explicit project approval;
5. perform the audit;
6. record the final decision in GitHub;
7. only then proceed to the next point.
