# P6.2.6 — Social, Sharing & Save Language v1

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 2026-08-18
**Phase:** P6.2 — Component Language

## Decision

Explore Luxembourg 360 is not a social network. Saving and sharing exist to support exploration, memory, planning and continuity.

## Account principle

> **Accountless exploration. Account-based memory.**

Exploration does not require an account. Save and personal memory do.

### No account required

- Explore the map
- Search and discovery
- View Places and Routes
- View Street View / 360° experiences
- Share content

### Account required

- Save a Place
- Save a Route
- Create and manage Collections
- My Explore
- Cloud synchronisation / cross-device continuity

This avoids a fragmented anonymous/local save system while keeping the initial exploration experience frictionless.

## Save

Save is a private, immediate action meaning:

> **I want to remember this place.**

Save is not a public popularity signal. The product does not introduce public like counts, follower systems or ranking based on likes.

## Collections

Collections organise saved discoveries into meaningful personal groups, for example:

- Weekend in Éislek
- Places I want to photograph
- Summer walks

Collections are part of My Explore and require an account.

The product may also provide separate editorial collections curated by Explore Luxembourg 360, such as seasonal discoveries, viewpoints or themed routes. Personal and editorial collections must remain conceptually distinct.

## Sharing

Sharing should be simple and should share the relevant content rather than merely the application:

- Place → share the Place
- Route → share the Route
- 360° Experience → share the experience
- Collection → future shareable collection capability, subject to a later decision

Deep links should preserve the relevant destination/context where technically appropriate.

## Continue Exploring

Saved content supports a continuous loop:

**Discover → Explore → Save → My Explore → Return → Continue Exploring**

Save, Collections and sharing must strengthen exploration rather than interrupt it.

## Visual principles

- Minimal controls.
- Save and Share are visible where context requires them.
- No social-media-style feed.
- No public comments, followers, likes or gamification in MVP.
- The visual language remains consistent with Place, Route and 360° components.

## Initial product fit

The feature is useful from launch with a relatively small catalogue because initial users can save routes, viewpoints, monuments and 360° experiences. Its value grows as the territory catalogue expands.

## Future readiness

The account model can later support richer synchronisation, shared collections, trip planning and other continuity features without requiring a separate anonymous save architecture.

## Explicit exclusions

The MVP excludes social feed, advanced community profiles, public comments, follower graphs, gamification and other community features outside the approved scope.

## Core principle

> **Save for yourself. Share with others.**

## Traceability

This decision is subordinate to the approved Product Vision, PDR-005 Screen Inventory + MVP User Flows, PDR-006 Visual Language and the existing architecture/product decisions.
