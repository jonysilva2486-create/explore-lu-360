# Explore Luxembourg 360 — R4.1 Mapping Provider Validation

**Status:** CLOSED / APPROVED  
**Parent:** R4 — External Infrastructure & Providers

## Purpose

Validate the mapping/provider strategy for the first vertical slice without reopening the approved provider-independent geospatial architecture.

## Core decision

Explore Luxembourg 360 will not make one external provider the definition of its geographic model. PostgreSQL/PostGIS remains authoritative for project-owned geographic data. External sources/providers are accessed through an internal geospatial abstraction boundary.

## Candidate roles

### Mapbox
Strong MVP candidate because current free tiers provide meaningful headroom for web map loads, Directions, Map Matching and temporary geocoding. Permanent geocoding is not treated as a free capability and must not become an implicit MVP dependency. Current official pricing confirms up to 50,000 Mapbox GL JS web map loads/month, 100,000 Directions requests/month, 100,000 Map Matching requests/month and 100,000 Temporary Geocoding requests/month at no charge; Permanent Geocoding has no free tier. 

### MapTiler
Strong technical candidate for vector/raster maps and related services. Its Free plan is suitable for testing/personal/non-commercial use, but is **not** treated as the commercial production solution. Commercial production requires the appropriate paid plan. Current Cloud pricing lists Flex at $30/month; Free usage pauses when its quota is exhausted. 

### Google Maps
Not the default basemap architecture. Google remains a strategic provider for Street View and may be used through Maps Embed for low-cost MVP integration where appropriate. Current Google pricing lists Maps Embed as unlimited free usage, while Dynamic Street View has a 5,000-event free usage cap and Street View Tiles 100,000 requests. Billable dynamic Google Maps APIs are introduced only when their additional control/capability is explicitly justified.

### OpenStreetMap
OSM data remains an important ecosystem/source. The public OSM tile servers are not treated as production tile infrastructure. Any OSM-derived service must respect its applicable attribution, caching, capacity and usage policies.

### Géoportail / ACT
Potential authoritative Luxembourg geographic layer. The project has contacted Géoportail/ACT to determine the appropriate technical and institutional integration route. This remains an external dependency and must not block unrelated R4 work.

## MUST HAVE — first vertical slice

- Web/mobile map rendering.
- Project-owned Place and Route geometries from PostGIS.
- Required official Luxembourg layers where available and permitted.
- Basic map interaction and required layers.
- User location display when permission is granted.
- A clean boundary to the Google Street View Experience.
- Provider abstraction so the map provider can be replaced without redesigning the domain model or R3 experience.

## SHOULD / FUTURE — not automatic MVP scope

- Advanced routing.
- Advanced geocoding/search.
- Permanent geocoding storage from third-party providers.
- Automated map matching beyond the concrete route-production need.
- Elevation services.
- 3D terrain.
- Advanced navigation.
- Provider-specific premium features.

A provider's feature catalogue must never silently expand product scope.

## Free-first / cost-controlled rule

During MVP validation, prefer free, open-source and free-tier options when they satisfy the approved requirements. Paid services are introduced only when a concrete product requirement, commercial licence, reliability need or scale constraint justifies the cost.

No recurring provider cost is assumed merely for convenience while project viability is still being validated.

## Provider abstraction rule

Provider-specific SDKs/APIs must not leak through Product/UI/domain contracts. The application should depend on internal capabilities such as map rendering, layers, geocoding or routing interfaces, with provider adapters behind that boundary.

## External validation status

Géoportail/ACT remains an external dependency. The initial contact has been made and the project will incorporate any relevant technical, licensing or institutional guidance when received. This pending response does not block closure of the architectural validation of R4.1 and does not constitute an assumed partnership, permission or licensing approval.

## Final decision state

No single basemap provider is mandated yet. Mapbox and MapTiler remain approved interchangeable candidates behind the abstraction boundary; Géoportail/ACT remains strategically important for official Luxembourg data; OSM remains a source/ecosystem rather than direct production tile infrastructure; Google Street View remains the principal external 360° Experience provider.

The implementation may proceed without prematurely locking the product to one basemap vendor. A concrete production provider selection can be made after technical proof and, where relevant, the Géoportail/ACT response.

The final validation considered:

1. technical fit against the R3 vertical slice;
2. cost at early and growth stages;
3. commercial/licensing conditions;
4. Luxembourg-specific data needs;
5. lock-in and provider replacement effort;
6. Google Street View integration and cost boundary;
7. the pending Géoportail/ACT response.

## Audit state

**R4.1 — FINAL AUDIT: PASS / CLOSED / APPROVED**

The approved result is an architectural decision, not a claim that one provider has been selected permanently. Provider selection remains an implementation/configuration decision constrained by this document.
