## Reconciled principles

### 1. Territory-first / not trail-first

Explore Luxembourg 360 is a territory exploration platform. Trails and routes are important entry points, but the product is not defined as a trail catalogue.

The 360 concept represents the territory as an interconnected matrix of:

- trails and routes;
- landscapes;
- places;
- nature;
- heritage;
- stories;
- POIs;
- experiences;
- immersive 360° media.

The relationship between these objects is part of the product value.

### 2. Exploration before administration

The core experience is:

`Explore → Discover → Explore → Continue`

The interface must support continuous discovery rather than feel like an administrative database or dashboard.

### 3. 360° as a product dimension

"360" is not only a panorama format. It expresses a broader, connected view of Luxembourg while also supporting immersive 360° experiences where appropriate.

The product must therefore avoid reducing the concept to a collection of 360° photographs.

### 4. Accountless exploration / account-based memory

Users can explore, search, view Places, Routes, maps and 360° experiences without an account. Authentication is introduced when persistent memory or synchronisation creates real value.

### 5. Premium editorial identity

The product must feel like a carefully designed digital atlas / editorial travel experience rather than a generic generated application.

Premium quality is achieved through restraint, typography, composition, cartographic context, photography, hierarchy and interaction quality rather than decorative effects.

### 6. Human and territorial character

The application should make Luxembourg feel present as a territory. Maps, imagery, stories, places and routes are treated as meaningful content, not interchangeable UI cards.

### 7. Community direction without premature social-network behaviour

Community knowledge and future contributions are part of the long-term product direction, but social-network mechanics, gamification and public profile systems are not allowed to distort the initial exploration experience.

### 8. Privacy as a product principle

Location is contextual and permission-based. Continuous/background tracking is excluded from the MVP. Data minimisation, account deletion, export, retention and transparent processing are architectural/product requirements rather than later compliance decoration.
