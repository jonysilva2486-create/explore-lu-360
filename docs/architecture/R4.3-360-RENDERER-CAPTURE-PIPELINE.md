# R4.3 — 360° Renderer & Capture Pipeline

## Status
**CLOSED / APPROVED — FINAL AUDIT PASS**

**Version:** v1 — August 2026

## Decision
Explore Luxembourg 360 will use Google Street View as the primary visual/distribution layer for project-created outdoor trail coverage, while maintaining an independent proprietary 360° Experience layer for selected immersive content.

The initial proprietary 360° renderer is **Photo Sphere Viewer** (open source, MIT). Google Street View remains the preferred ecosystem for continuous trail coverage created by the project itself. The two are exposed through a common Explore 360 Experience abstraction.

## Trail coverage principle

The project does **not** assume that suitable Street View coverage already exists for Luxembourg trails. The project will create its own outdoor 360° trail coverage.

The relationship remains:

**Route → GPS geometry → project capture → Google Street View coverage**

Google provides the publication/distribution and viewing infrastructure. Explore Luxembourg 360 remains authoritative for Route identity, GPS geometry, Experience relationships, Places, Stories and territorial context.

## 360° Capture Standard v1

### Pass 1 — Trail Capture

Purpose: **document the trail**.

- Record the route continuously in 360°.
- Keep the capture sequence as uninterrupted as practical.
- Maintain the required location/GPS information.
- Mark interesting points without normally interrupting the trail capture.
- Do not stop the main capture for every monument/viewpoint/photo opportunity.

Exception: if an exceptional opportunity occurs (for example unusually good light), a short controlled interruption is allowed and must be noted for quality control.

### Backup

- Preserve original camera files.
- Copy video, GPS and metadata before processing.
- Never work only from the single original.
- Maintain a recoverable master copy before publication.

### Quality Control

Check:
- continuity;
- image quality;
- GPS accuracy/availability;
- file integrity;
- missing/problematic sections;
- privacy/sensitive content where applicable.

Only approved material proceeds to publication/processing.

### Google Street View publication

Outdoor 360° trail video is prepared for Google Street View Studio. Supported video formats and GPS/GPX handling must be verified against Google's current requirements at the time of the first real capture. Processing time is not assumed to be immediate; published panorama connections may take several days to become available.

Workflow:

**Capture → Backup → QC → Upload → Google Processing → Verify → Integrate**

### Pass 2 — Experience Capture

Purpose: **interpret the trail**.

Return to selected locations after the continuous trail capture and create deliberate editorial/immersive assets:

- special 360° panoramas;
- photographs;
- details;
- viewpoints;
- heritage/monument documentation;
- nature content;
- video where relevant;
- Story material.

This second pass allows time to compose, wait for better conditions and capture content without compromising the continuity of the trail recording.

### Optional targeted re-capture

If QC identifies a problem or a location deserves substantially better coverage, re-capture only the affected segment/location rather than repeating the entire route.

## Experience integration

A Route may contain a continuous project-created Street View sequence plus selected proprietary Experiences:

```text
Route
 ├── Trail Street View panorama sequence
 ├── Special 360 Experience
 ├── Story
 ├── Place
 └── Heritage / Nature context
```

The user flow may be:

**Street View trail → special Explore 360 Experience → Return to Trail**

Google's current Street View JavaScript capabilities support custom panoramas and links between panoramas, including links between custom content and Street View. The exact implementation path (native custom panorama integration versus controlled application transition) remains a technical PoC decision and must not be assumed until tested.

## Renderer strategy

### Google Street View
Use the Google Street View ecosystem for project-created continuous outdoor trail coverage and broader Street View content where appropriate.

### Proprietary 360°
Use Photo Sphere Viewer for independent Explore 360 panoramas and special immersive experiences, especially where the content is editorial, indoor or otherwise better controlled by the project.

### Independence rule
Google is a preferred bridge/distribution layer, not the owner of the Explore 360 Experience model. Proprietary Experiences must remain renderable independently of Google.

## Outdoor vs indoor rule

The standard Street View Studio trail pipeline is intended for outdoor trail coverage. Indoor/special content should normally use the proprietary Explore 360 renderer unless a separate, explicitly validated Google-compatible publishing workflow is chosen.

## Cost strategy

**Embed-first.**

Use the lowest-cost Google integration that satisfies the experience. Google Maps Embed is preferred where its capabilities are sufficient. More controllable APIs such as Maps JavaScript API / Dynamic Street View are introduced only when a validated product requirement justifies their use and the associated cost.

Do not use Static Street View as the primary interactive 360° renderer.

## Data ownership and identity

Explore Luxembourg 360 must retain its own identifiers for:

- Route;
- Route geometry;
- capture session;
- Experience;
- location/coordinates;
- sequence position;
- Places/Stories/territorial relationships.

A Google panorama ID is an **external reference, not a permanent territorial identifier**. Google panorama references may change and must never be the sole durable identifier for a Route or Experience. Coordinates and project-owned identifiers remain authoritative.

## Future 3D capability — NOT MVP

The Insta360 X6 may be investigated in the future as a possible source of data useful for 3D/immersive experiences, particularly for castles, monuments and interiors.

This is a **future research capability**, not an MVP requirement and not a claim that the X6 currently provides a complete navigable 3D pipeline. It must not alter the main 360° capture workflow.

## Fundamental principles

1. **Capture the trail first; create the special content second.**
2. **Preserve originals before processing.**
3. **Quality before publication.**
4. **Route identity and territorial relationships remain ours.**
5. **Google is infrastructure, not the Explore 360 domain model.**
6. **The user experience must remain continuous and intuitive.**
7. **Free-first / cost-controlled integration is mandatory.**
8. **Every field capture should generate useful project assets, not content produced solely for a third party.**
9. **Future 3D is preserved as an option without contaminating the MVP scope.**

## First-field validation

Capture Standard v1 is the approved baseline, but the first real route capture is a required field validation event. Findings may produce a v1.1 refinement without reopening the underlying R4.3 architecture unless a genuine architectural issue is discovered.

## Final audit result

**PASS / CLOSED / APPROVED**

The audit confirmed the renderer strategy, two-pass capture pipeline, project-created Street View model, proprietary 360° layer, Street View ↔ Explore 360 transition requirement, outdoor/indoor distinction, cost guardrails, project-owned territorial identity and future 3D placeholder.
